# SEO Keyword Map — javeabliss.com

Research date: August 2026. Method: live SERP analysis per market (what ranks, exact competitor
title tags, autocomplete/related patterns) — not paid keyword tools, so treat as evidence-based
direction, not volume data.

## Cross-market findings (apply everywhere)

1. **"250 m from Arenal Beach" is the winning claim.** Every market's rankers lead with
   distance-to-Arenal; almost all say vague "walking distance". Exact metres beats them.
   Arenal is the only sub-location with dedicated search demand — and single-property sites
   demonstrably rank for "town + Arenal + apartment" in every language.
2. **Direct booking is a searched intent, not just a pitch.** UK: "book direct / owners direct /
   no booking fees"; DE: "von privat / provisionsfrei"; FR: "entre particuliers / sans commission";
   NL: "van de eigenaar / zonder commissie"; ES: "sin comisiones"; IT: "direttamente dai
   proprietari / senza commissioni". Say it in those exact words.
3. **Winter long stays are a mature, named market** fitting the €100/night 35+ nights rate:
   EN "winter let / winter rentals", NL "overwinteren", DE "Überwintern / Langzeitmiete",
   FR "location hivernale / hivernage", ES "alquiler larga temporada", IT "affitto lungo periodo".
   Biggest under-served opportunity across all six markets.
4. **The head terms ("holiday rentals Javea") are owned by OTAs** (Booking, Vrbo, Holidu,
   Casamundo). Winnable territory: apartment + Arenal + capacity/feature long-tail, direct-booking
   intent, and winter-stay intent.
5. **Spelling:** users type "javea" unaccented everywhere; render "Jávea" on-page (Google folds
   accents), mention "(Xàbia)" once per page. Facts sell: exact metres, exact prices, exact counts.

## 🇬🇧 English (UK/Ireland primary)

- **Primary:** holiday apartment Javea (+ "near Arenal Beach")
- **Secondary:** holiday rentals in Javea · self-catering apartment Javea · apartments to rent
  in Javea · Arenal Beach apartment · winter rentals Javea / winter let · book direct no booking fees
- **Long-tail themes:** winter sun long stays (300+ sun days, 16–18 °C winters, town open all
  year); flights — ALC from all major UK airports + Dublin (easyJet, Ryanair, Jet2, BA), 2.5–3 h
  then ~75–90 min transfer; family beach holidays (sleeps 4, shallow water, playground, no car
  needed); Javea vs Moraira comparisons (forum-dominated, content gap)
- **Title:** `Javea Holiday Apartment near Arenal Beach | Book Direct`
- **Description:** `Self-catering 2-bed apartment 250 m from Arenal Beach, Javea. Sleeps 4, air
  con, fast WiFi, lift & free parking. Book direct & save from €130/night. Winter stays welcome.`
- **Idiom:** "holiday" never "vacation"; "self-catering"; "to rent" not "for rent"; "sleeps 4";
  "air con"; "winter let". Spell "Javea" in EN content.

## What was removed and why (technical cleanup, Aug 2026)

- Six SEO components fought over `document.title`/meta (SEOHead, StructuredData,
  CountryTargetedSEO, DiscountSEO, LastMinuteSEO, OptimizedSEOStrategy) → consolidated into
  SEOHead only. DiscountSEO hardcoded a permanent fake "🔥 20% OFF LIMITED TIME" title.
- Removed: 300-term keyword meta tags (ignored since 2009), fake meta tags (weather-keywords,
  travel.airlines, geo-targeting city lists — no engine reads these), hidden `sr-only` SEO text
  (hidden-text guideline violation), fake aggregateRating 4.9/47 + self-awarded 5-star rating
  in JSON-LD (structured-data spam risk), fake phone "+34-XXX-XXX-XXX", fake SearchAction,
  conflicting geo coordinates (4 different sets) and room counts (2 vs 3).
- Promotional offers now render a clean localized title variant ("20% Off – …") from the real
  /api/promotional-offers data, plus a legitimate schema.org Offer.
