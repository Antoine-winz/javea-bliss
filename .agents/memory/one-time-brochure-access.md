---
name: One-time brochure access
description: The private sales brochure requires a fresh registration for every copy.
---

Allow exactly one brochure download after each successful registration and email verification, then invalidate the access session.

**Why:** The owner requires each copy and each device access to be associated with a fresh potential-buyer registration.

**How to apply:** Do not preserve brochure-download access after a successful download. A new copy requires the visitor to register and verify again.
